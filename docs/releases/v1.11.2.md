对话视图右侧新增自适应比例滚动条，翻阅长对话与待审批 diff 时位置一目了然。

- 对话比例滚动条：当对话内容超出可视高度时，在右侧边距自动显示轨道（`│`）与按可视比例缩放的滑块（`┃`）；跟随底部时滑块低调静默，向上翻阅历史时滑块自动高亮为强调色，并在后台继续流式输出时实时更新比例而不跳动画面。
- 支持点击与拖拽定位：在开启终端鼠标上报的普通终端中，可直接点击或按住拖动右侧滚动条快速跳转到对话任意位置；在开启原生文字划选的桌面常驻窗口中，滚动条随滚轮与键盘平滑同步。

Added a proportional scrollbar to the conversation viewport so your position in long chats and pending approval diffs is visible at a glance.

- Proportional transcript scrollbar: when messages exceed the viewport height, a track (`│`) and proportionally sized thumb (`┃`) appear along the right margin. The thumb stays subtle while following the bottom, highlights in accent colour while reviewing earlier history, and updates smoothly if background streaming appends new lines.
- Click and drag navigation: in terminals with mouse reporting enabled, clicking or dragging the scrollbar jumps directly to the corresponding position; in the desktop window with native mouse text selection, the scrollbar tracks mouse-wheel and keyboard scrolling smoothly.
